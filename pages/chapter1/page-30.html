Multiple Interrupts

多重中断

Excerpted from “Ch01v2a.pdf” — Page 30

Original Content (verbatim)

  • An interrupt occurs while another interrupt is being processed在处理一个中断时又发生了另一个中断
  • e.g. receiving data from a communications line and printing results at the same time例如：在从通信线路接收数据的同时打印结果
  • Two approaches:两种方法：
  • Disable interrupts while an interrupt is being processed在处理中断期间禁止中断
  • Use a priority scheme使用优先级方案
